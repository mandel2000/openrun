# Code of conduct

OpenRun follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Running is for every body, and so is this project.

**Expected**: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions and experience levels, constructive feedback given and accepted gracefully, owning our mistakes, focusing on what is best for the community. Advice on training and health is given carefully and without shaming.

**Not acceptable**: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information (including their training or health data) without explicit permission, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's *Report content* option on the comment or issue, or open a private security report (*Security* tab) marked "Code of conduct". Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public spaces.
